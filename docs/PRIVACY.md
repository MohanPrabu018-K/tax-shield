# Privacy — TaxShield

## Pages & controls (existing static pages + planned settings)
Privacy Policy (`/privacy`), Terms (`/terms`), Cookie/Data-Processing/Security/
Retention/Deletion policies. Consent split: essential service vs optional
analytics / AI processing / communications / marketing — never one bundled checkbox.

## Rights
Export My Data (CSV/JSON/PDF), account/workspace deletion (auth + confirm +
grace + audit + no orphaned docs), consent history, session list + logout-all,
privacy settings incl. AI DISABLED / Local-only default.

## AI privacy
Users are told when AI is used. Customer financials never train models unless
explicitly opted in with valid legal basis. Training uses synthetic/public/
anonymized data only; temporaries deleted after jobs (see `docs/AI_POLICY.md`).
