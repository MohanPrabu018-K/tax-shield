## 5. COMPREHENSIVE PILOT FAQ

### Q1: Is the 60-day pilot genuinely free?
**A:** Yes. Participation is 100% complimentary. There are no license fees, setup charges, or user seat fees. We do NOT ask for credit card or payment details during registration. At Day 60, your account will never auto-bill or auto-convert.

### Q2: How does TaxShield protect our client data?
**A:** Your firm retains 100% exclusive ownership of all data. TaxShield maintains strict multi-tenant isolation, database-level segregation, and RFC 6238 TOTP Multi-Factor Authentication. We explicitly warrant that client financial and tax data is **never used to train AI models** and is never shared with third parties.

### Q3: How does data migration with Tally Prime work?
**A:** TaxShield includes a native XML parser for Tally Prime. You can export Master Ledgers or Journal Vouchers from Tally Prime as standard XML and import them into TaxShield in seconds. The importer automatically checks for balanced debits/credits and suppresses duplicate vouchers.

### Q4: Does TaxShield comply with ICAI professional guidelines?
**A:** Yes. The platform was architected around ICAI requirements:
- **Confidentiality:** Aligns with Clause 1 of Part I of the Second Schedule.
- **Quality Control (SQC-1):** Dual-control review gates prevent junior preparers from approving tasks without partner review.
- **Documentation (SA 230):** Standardized workpapers and UDIN tracking ensure complete audit trails.
- **Client Portals:** Operate as private, password-protected workspaces for existing clients only, adhering to ICAI website guidelines.

### Q5: What happens at the end of the 60-day pilot if we do not wish to continue?
**A:** You can export all your firm and client data (full JSON databases, spreadsheets, and Tally XML vouchers) at any time. Upon your instruction, our automated exit protocol cryptographically purges your workspace and delivers a formal Certificate of Destruction.
